The High Sheriff’s Community Conferences (HSCC) on Mental Health were established to create a safe, practical and locally grounded space for conversations about mental health across West Sussex. Bringing together voluntary and community organisations, statutory services, public health, education, emergency services and people with lived experience, the conferences were designed to complement the introduction of the NHS Neighbourhood Mental Health Service and Pathfinder service, while strengthening collaboration at neighbourhood level.

Three consistent priority themes emerged — access to services, stigma, and men’s mental health — however, the open, place-based, non-hierarchical structure of the conferences enabled a much wider range of interconnected issues to be explored.

The report captures both the insights generated and some of the practical next steps now underway. Above all, it calls for a more connected, responsive and equitable system — one that builds on the remarkable commitment already present across West Sussex and translates shared learning into sustained, measurable change.
