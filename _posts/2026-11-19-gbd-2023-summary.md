This page provides an overview of the latest iteration of the Global Burden of Disease and Risk Factors Study (GBD) 2023, published in October 2025. To support colleagues within public health and the general public to learn more about the study, supplementary resources including a summary document and frequently asked questions guide are also provided. 

Alongside an overview and learning resources, the Public Health and Social Research Unit have been working on a series of briefings which summarise findings from GBD 2023 across a variety of areas such as cardiovascular disease, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and mental disorders for the South East and England.
